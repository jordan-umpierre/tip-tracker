# Privacy Policy for Tip Tracker

Effective 2026-08-11.

Tip Tracker is a local-only iOS shift and tip logging app. This policy
describes what the app stores and where it goes.

## The short version

- The app stores your data in SQLite on your device.
- The app does not require an account, network connection, or server.
- The app does not collect email addresses, analytics, advertising IDs, or
  crash data.
- The app does not sell or share your data.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device:

- jobs, hourly rates, and optional overtime settings
- shift dates, times, duration, tips, notes, and the rate used for each shift
- optional federal withholding settings
- local backups and imported data only when you choose those file actions

Money is stored as integer cents and duration as integer seconds. The
withholding estimator's temporary wage input and result are not stored.

## Files you export

CSV and JSON backups are written only when you choose an export location or
share destination. Those files are under your control. Tip Tracker does not
upload them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its local database according to the operating
system's app-data behavior. Individual jobs and shifts can be removed inside
the app. Exported files must be deleted from the location where you saved them.

## Children

Tip Tracker is meant for people who work for wages and is not directed to
children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data behavior changes, this policy and the App Store privacy
answers must be updated before that behavior ships.
