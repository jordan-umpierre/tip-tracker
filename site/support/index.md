# Tip Tracker support

For help with Tip Tracker, email
[umpierrejordan@gmail.com](mailto:umpierrejordan@gmail.com).

Please describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Do
not email exported shift histories, backups, or other sensitive financial
information.

Tip Tracker has no account or cloud service. Your jobs, shifts, settings, and
backups remain under your control on your device or in locations you choose.

## Removing app data

You can remove individual jobs and shifts inside the app. Uninstalling Tip
Tracker removes its local database according to iOS app-data behavior. Delete
exported CSV or JSON files from the location where you saved them.
